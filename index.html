About us

a little cafe with
big dreams.

Le Rêve is a specialty cafe in Malindi serving layered iced lattes, thick milkshakes and fresh bakes — the kind of place built for slow mornings and golden-hour photos.

Everything on the menu is made to order — from our famous Iced Strawberry Latte to the Biscoff Donut that never survives the afternoon. We pour slow, we plate pretty, and we keep the case full of fresh treats.

You'll find us at Imadi Shopping Complex, open every day from 11am. Order to go on WhatsApp and we'll have it ready by the time you arrive, or come sit with us and watch the coast go by.
